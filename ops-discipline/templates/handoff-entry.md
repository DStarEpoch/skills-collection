# Handoff-document entry skeleton

> Append-only, anchored, monotonically numbered. Facts and inferences stay separate;
> every conclusion carries an evidence pointer; open items are never quietly closed.

```
<a id="<topic>-<date>"></a>
#### §<number> <title> (<local time with offset>, <author>)

**Facts** (measured / verbatim log lines / identity SHAs):
- ...

**Classification** (what kind of problem/phenomenon this is, and why):
- ...

**Ruling / actions** (what was done, who authorized it, requirements assigned to whom):
- ...

**Open** (unverified, waiting on whom, what to check next):
- ...
```
